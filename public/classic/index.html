How it works

  1. Join by buying $CULT. Joining is free: no tax on buys.
  2. Leaving costs 10%: the exit tithe, split between the members who stay.
  3. Every 10 minutes, the tithe is swapped to USDC and split between members.
  4. Hold $50 or more to be counted. Stay longer and your devotion earns up to 1.5× the cut.
